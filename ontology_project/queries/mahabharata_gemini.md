# Mahabharata Queries (Gemini Pro)

**Focus**: Kuru lineage (Pandavas, Kauravas, elders), key events (Game of Dice, Exile, Kurukshetra War), and locations (Hastinapura, Indraprastha).

Use the **Unified Gemini Pro Template** from `templates.md` for all queries.

---

## Queries

1. "Model the Kuru royal family genealogy: parents, siblings, consorts, and children for the **Pandavas** (Yudhishthira, Bhima, Arjuna, Nakula, Sahadeva) and their common consort **Draupadi**."
2. "Model the Kuru lineage elders: **Bhishma** (Devavrata), **Dhritarashtra**, **Pandu**, and **Vidura**, including their parentage, titles, and relationships."
3. "Model the **Kauravas** ( Duryodhana, Dushasana) and their relationships to their parents (Dhritarashtra, Gandhari) and preceptors (**Drona**, **Kripa**)."
4. "Extract the event of the **Game of Dice** (Dyuta) and the subsequent **Exile of the Pandavas** (Vana Parva and Virata Parva), linking locations visited."
5. "Model the **Kurukshetra War** (Kurukshetra) as an Event, including key commanders of both sides (Pandava and Kaurava armies), duration, and outcome."
6. "Extract the characters **Karna** and **Kunti**, including their mother-son relationship, Karna's adoption, and his alliance with Duryodhana."
7. "Model the sacred geography of **Hastinapura** and **Indraprastha** as Places, explaining their political and narrative significance."
8. "Extract the characters **Veda Vyasa** (the composer) and **Ganesha** (the scribe), and their roles in recording the Mahabharata text."
9. "Model the concept of **Vidura Niti** (ethical teachings of Vidura) and its connection to Dhritarashtra and Yudhishthira."
10. "Model the event of **Krishna's Peace Embassy** (Krishna Dutu) to Hastinapura, detailing participants, the proposal, and the rejection by Duryodhana."
