# Vedas Queries (Gemini Pro)

**Focus**: The four Vedas and their sub-sections, the Vedic pantheon (Agni, Indra, Varuna, Soma), and primary philosophical concepts (Rta, Yajna, Purusha Sukta).

Use the **Unified Gemini Pro Template** from `templates.md` for all queries.

---

## Queries

1. "Model the structural division of the **Vedas** (Rigveda, Samaveda, Yajurveda, Atharveda) and their four layers (**Samhita**, **Brahmana**, **Aranyaka**, **Upanishad**)."
2. "Model the Vedic deity **Agni** (Fire God) as a Deity: attributes, role as messenger/mouth of gods in Yajna, and relationships."
3. "Model the Vedic deity **Indra** (Rain/Thunder God) as a Deity: weapons (Vajra), vehicle (Airavata), and battles (Vritra slaying)."
4. "Model the Vedic deity **Varuna** (Cosmic Order God) and his relationship to **Rta** (universal truth/moral order)."
5. "Model the Vedic deity **Soma** (Moon/Soma juice God): attributes, association with plants, and connection to the moon."
6. "Extract the concept of **Yajna** (Vedic sacrifice) and the four classes of priests (**Hotri**, **Adhvaryu**, **Udgatri**, **Brahman**) who conduct it."
7. "Model the **Purusha Sukta** hymn of Rigveda Chapter 10, detailing the cosmic sacrifice of **Purusha** and the origin of the four varnas."
8. "Extract the philosophical concept of **Rta** (natural and moral order of the cosmos) and its relationship to **Dharma**."
9. "Model the Vedic deities **Surya** (Sun God) and the twin **Ashvins** (divine physicians): attributes, vehicles, and roles."
10. "Model the **Prasthana Trayi** (Three Sources) linking the Upanishads (Vedas), Brahma Sutras, and Bhagavad Gita as standard texts."
