# Puranas Queries (Gemini Pro)

**Focus**: Puranic cosmology (Lokas, Meru), cycles of time (Yugas, Manvantaras), creation myths (Srishti, Pralaya), and major sectarian legends (Samudra Manthan, Daksha Yajna).

Use the **Unified Gemini Pro Template** from `templates.md` for all queries.

---

## Queries

1. "Classify and model the **18 Mahapuranas** (Great Puranas) as Texts, grouping them by their primary dedication to Brahma, Vishnu, or Shiva."
2. "Model Puranic cosmology: the **14 Lokas** (7 upper realms like Bhu-loka, Svarga, Satya-loka; and 7 netherworlds like Atala, Patala), showing their hierarchy."
3. "Model the sacred abodes **Vaikuntha** (Vishnu's abode) and **Mount Kailash** (Shiva's abode) as Places, including their residents and descriptions."
4. "Model the cycles of time: the **Chatur Yugas** (Satya, Treta, Dvapara, Kali) and **Manvantaras** (ruled by Manus), showing durations and characteristics."
5. "Extract the Puranic creation myth of **Srishti** (creation by Brahma emerging from Vishnu's navel lotus) and the dissolution of the universe (**Pralaya**)."
6. "Model the legendary event of **Samudra Manthan** (Churning of the Milky Ocean), detailing the participants (Devas, Asuras), the mountain Meru/Mandara, the tortoise Kurma, and the items that emerged."
7. "Extract the legend of the **Daksha Yajna** (Daksha's sacrifice) and the self-immolation of **Sati**, showing relationships to Shiva, Daksha, and Sati."
8. "Model the avatars and manifestations of Shiva: **Veerabhadra** and **Bhairava**, showing their purposes and origins."
9. "Model the legend of **Dhruva** and his penance to attain the status of the Pole Star (**Dhruva-loka**), showing relationships to Vishnu and Suniti."
10. "Extract the legends of **Markandeya** sage, his devotion to Shiva, and how Shiva saved him from **Yama** (God of Death)."
